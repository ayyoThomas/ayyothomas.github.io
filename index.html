The Forgotten Files

True stories history left in the footnotes.

The Forgotten Files is a YouTube channel that tells little-known true stories from history: the people, decisions and moments that shaped the world but rarely made it into the textbooks.

Every video is researched from published sources, and the sources are listed in each video's description. Narration is produced with AI voice technology, and some illustrations are AI-generated; archive photographs come from public-domain and openly licensed collections.
