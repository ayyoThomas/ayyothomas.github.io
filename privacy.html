Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the publishing tool ("history channel") that The Forgotten Files uses to upload videos to its own YouTube channel.

Who uses it

The tool is used only by the owner of The Forgotten Files channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not let anyone else sign in.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing times, and read basic information about the owner's own channel. It does not access other people's accounts, channels or personal data.

What it stores

The sign-in token and information about the channel's own videos are stored privately on the owner's server and are used only to publish and manage those videos. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Viewers

The tool does not collect any information about people who watch the channel. Viewing videos on YouTube is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted when the tool is retired or on request.
